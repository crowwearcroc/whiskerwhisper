What I help with

Cats don't misbehave out of spite. Behavior is how they tell us something is wrong: stress, a setup that doesn't suit them, or a need that isn't being met. These are the problems I see most.

  • Peeing or pooping outside the litter boxThe most common reason people call, and very fixable.
  • Scratching furniture, carpets or peopleRedirecting the urge instead of fighting it.
  • Cats who don't get alongHissing, chasing, blocking and full-on fights.
  • Biting or swattingIncluding the cat who bites in the middle of being petted.
  • Hiding, fear and stressHelping shy or anxious cats feel safe at home.
  • Yowling at nightGetting everyone in the house some sleep.
  • Big changesMoving, a new baby, a new partner or a new pet.
  • Introducing a new catSetting up a slow, calm introduction from day one.

Many behavior changes start with a health problem, like a urinary infection or pain. If your cat's behavior has changed suddenly, please see your vet first. I'm happy to work alongside them.

How it works

  1. Free 15-minute call

    Tell me what's going on. We'll decide together whether a consultation is the right next step.

  2. A short questionnaire

    You'll share your cat's history, daily routine and home layout. Photos and videos of the problem help a lot.

  3. Consultation

    We meet in your home or by video. I watch how your cat moves through the space and talk through what's happening.

  4. Your written plan

    Clear, step-by-step changes to try, written for your cat and your household.

  5. Follow-up

    We check in over the following weeks and adjust the plan as your cat responds.
